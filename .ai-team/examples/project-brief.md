# Customer support portal

Build a web portal where existing customers can create support tickets, see replies
and follow their ticket status. Support staff can assign, reply to and close tickets.

## Goals

- Responsive, accessible customer and staff views.
- Existing repository authentication conventions remain in use.
- Customers can only access their own tickets.
- Tests cover authorization and the create/reply/close journey.

## Constraints

- Inspect the repository before choosing libraries or replacing existing patterns.
- Keep the first release bounded: attachments, billing and live chat are out of scope.
- Use local test fixtures; never include real credentials or customer data.
- Each task must have measurable acceptance criteria and validation.
